Introductory Microbiology

Moraine Valley Community College · Biology · In person, 4 credit hours

Welcome to microbiology

You will explore the bacteria, archaea, fungi, protozoa, algae, helminths, viruses, prions, and viroids that shape health and disease. Each week pairs a lecture lesson with a hands-on lab, and your labs build toward identifying an unknown bacterium using data and evidence. Along the way you will build scientific literacy, study habits, and aseptic technique for future nursing and health programs.

  • In person
  • 3 h lecture + 3 h lab weekly
  • 16 weeks
  • 16 activities
  • 16 labs
  • 6 exams

Assessment overview

You can earn 1,000 points. The course has six unit exams and no cumulative final. All exams and practicals are in person and proctored. Lowest scores are dropped for homework, lab worksheets, pre-lab quizzes, and in-class activities, so a short illness will not sink your grade.

Syllabus, schedule, and policies

Your full syllabus, current-term schedule, and due dates are in Canvas. Highlights:

  • Lab safety: Treat every organism as a possible pathogen. Wear your lab coat, long pants, closed-toe shoes, and safety glasses. Never eat, drink, or use your phone in lab, and never remove cultures from the lab.
  • Attendance: Lab attendance is mandatory. Tell your instructor about an absence as soon as you can.
  • Late work: Documented excuses are handled with your instructor. Unexcused late work loses 5% per day. In-class activities have no make-ups.
  • Academic integrity: Discuss lab answers with lab mates, but write them in your own words. Using AI is fine for studying, but presenting it as your own work is a violation.
  • Access and support: Contact the Center for Disability Services early, because accommodations are not retroactive. Free tutoring is available through the Academic Skills Center.
  • Materials: Free OpenStax textbook, lab manual, lab coat, gloves, and glasses (covered by course fees), plus a dark Sharpie and a bag. Bring a web-enabled device for Poll Everywhere.
